Spacing & radii

A 4-pt rhythm and a four-stop radius scale keep the whole popover coherent at a glance. Corners nest: an inner element always rounds tighter than the surface holding it, so concentric edges stay parallel.

Compact, not cramped. Rows run ~44 pt tall with 11×14 padding; buttons hold a 28 pt minimum even inside the dense popover. The menubar context rewards tight vertical rhythm, but nothing interactive drops below a comfortable target.

Concentric corners. The r6 favicon sits inside an r10 row inside the r14 popover — each step tighter so the rounded edges read as parallel, never mismatched.
