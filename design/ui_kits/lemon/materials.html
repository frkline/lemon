Materials & elevation

Lemon is built from one material at four thicknesses. Hierarchy is the blur and the fill opacity — a surface comes forward by thickening, not by gaining a border or a drop shadow. Only the popover itself casts a shadow; everything inside floats on material alone.

Regular

Thicken to advance
Hover and selection swap thin → regular: more blur, a denser fill, a slightly brighter hairline. The element reads as nearer without moving or growing.
Half-pixel hairlines
Edges are inset 0 0 0 .5px rgba(255,255,255,.07–.12) — a catch of light along the top, never a 1 px border. Dividers are the same idea at .08–.12 alpha.
Shadow is for the window, not the parts
The popover drops 0 22px 56px rgba(0,0,0,.5). Inner surfaces never stack their own shadows — depth there is purely material.
The machine surface is solid
Console and terminal are opaque warm-black. Glass is for chrome the user touches; verbatim machine output sits on something that doesn’t shift with the desktop behind it.
