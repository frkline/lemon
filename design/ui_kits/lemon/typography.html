Typography

Two families, one job each. SF Pro Text carries everything a person writes or reads; SF Mono carries everything a machine reports — issue keys, repo paths, tokens, and console output. The split is the system: if it’s monospaced, it came back verbatim from Linear, GitHub, or the agent.

Set tight. Display sizes carry −0.1 to −0.5 letter-spacing; the all-caps Label is the one exception, opened to +1.4 so it reads as a quiet section marker, not a shout. Minimum sizes: 10 px for labels and machine IDs, 11–13 px for anything a person actually reads. Nothing smaller ships.
