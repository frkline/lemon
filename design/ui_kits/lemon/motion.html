Motion

Motion exists to explain where something came from and what it belongs to — never to decorate. The popover opens with one short, staggered rise; after that the interface holds still until the user acts. Nothing loops.

Specification

rise
Staggered entrance
translateY 7px → 0, opacity 0 → 1 over .52s on cubic-bezier(.2,.75,.2,1). Children stagger by ~80ms, top to bottom, so the list assembles from the menubar downward.
.2s
Chevron & expand
The focus chevron rotates 90° over .2s as a session expands in place. The relationship is the point: the terminal grows out of the row it belongs to.
swap
Hover & selection
State changes swap material (thin → regular) rather than animating color. Fast and physical — a surface coming forward, not a tween.
live
The one allowed loop
Only a running agent may animate continuously, and only as a soft pulse on its live dot. Everything else is still.
A11y

Every animation is gated on prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference. The visible end-state is the base style, so reduced-motion, print, and first paint all show finished content — never a stuck opacity:0.
