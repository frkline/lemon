# Liquid Glass pass — frkline/lemon#14

Moved every Lemon chrome surface onto a single source of truth:
`LemonGlass` view modifier + `GlassElevation` tokens in `LemonDesign.swift`.

## What changed

- **`LemonDesign.swift`** — new tokens (`glassTintLemon`, `glassTintCoral`,
  `glassTintLinear`, `glassTintGitHub`, `glassEdge`, `glassEdgeHover`),
  `LD.GlassElevation` enum (`.resting | .hover | .selected | .floating`),
  and `LemonGlass` `ViewModifier` plus `.lemonGlass(_:)` / `.lemonGlassCapsule(_:)`
  helpers. `.resting` is `.thinMaterial` + soft edge; `.hover` thickens to
  `.regularMaterial` with a lemon glaze; `.selected` casts a source-tinted
  glow under the material; `.floating` uses `.ultraThinMaterial` composition
  for pills/action rows (composes correctly in both the prod menubar window
  and the smoke-test `NSHostingView` — plain `.glassEffect()` rendered as
  opaque white outside a Liquid-Glass-capable container).
- **`StatusPill`** + **`SourceGlyph`** — flat capsule fills replaced with
  `.lemonGlassCapsule(.floating, tint:)`. Status / provider colours now glow
  under the glass rather than stroking around it.
- **`PopoverView`** — softened the 2px lemon-yellow top bar into a 4px
  lensing gradient (`LD.lemon → clear`). Root background gains a faint
  lemon glaze on top of `.regularMaterial`. The detail-footer action row
  (`Join` / `Stop`) gets the floating glass treatment so it hovers over
  the opaque console. Empty-state watching rows pick up source-tinted glass.
- **`SessionRowView`** — resting rows now sit on `.thinMaterial` with a
  source-tinted overlay (Linear → honey-amber, GitHub → green). Hover
  thickens to `.regularMaterial` + lemon glaze — depth comes from the
  material swap, not a heavier stroke.
- **`SettingsView`** — every ad-hoc `.background(.primary.opacity(0.04), in:
  RoundedRectangle…)` panel now consumes `.lemonGlass(.resting)`. The
  `+ Add` chip + `Re-run setup` chip switch to `.lemonGlassCapsule(.resting)`.
  `PairRowView` rest/hover routes through the modifier with validation-coral
  tint for error states.
- **`IdentityEditorPane`** + **`WorkspaceEditorPane`** — pane backgrounds get
  a faint lemon-tinted glaze (the "second room" reading), all interior
  panels (surfaces summary, routed-by, folder-layout) move to
  `.lemonGlass(.resting)`. The identity-choice picker's selected state
  uses source-tinted glass + a source-coloured check mark — keeps lemon
  yellow exclusive to the actual primary CTA (Save), per the brand
  discipline.

## Out of scope (per the issue body)

`OnboardingView.swift` (deliberately left at `.regularMaterial` —
issue calls out the onboarding chrome as separate from the post-onboarding
popover work). The legacy `WorkspaceEditorView` sheet form is also
untouched — it's no longer in the navigation graph.
